FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is Mellow free for job seekers?+
Yes, completely free for candidates. Always. We charge employers only when a hire is made.
Do I need professional equipment to record?+
Not at all. Your phone is enough. Good lighting and a clear background go a long way, but authenticity matters more than production quality.
Can I apply to multiple jobs with the same video?+
Yes, that's exactly the point. Record once and apply to every role with a single tap. No re-recording, no cover letters.
Which countries is Mellow available in?+
Mellow is open to talent and employers everywhere.
How does Mellow make money?+
Mellow is free to start for everyone. Candidates are always free. Employers can post their first role and browse the talent feed at zero cost. When employers hire regularly, they upgrade to a simple monthly subscription for unlimited roles, advanced filters, and team seats.
