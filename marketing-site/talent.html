FAQ for talent

Your questions answered

Is Mellow really free for talent?+
Yes, completely free. Always. Talent never pays anything on Mellow. We charge employers a monthly subscription when they hire regularly. Your side of the platform will always be free.
What do I say in my 60-second video?+
We give you three prompts: who you are and your background, what you're genuinely good at, and what kind of role you're looking for. Keep it natural, the best profiles are conversational, not rehearsed.
Can I update my video later?+
Yes. Your profile is yours to update whenever you want.
What if I'm not comfortable on camera?+
Most people feel that way before they record. The prompts help a lot. You're not performing, you're just having a conversation. Employers actually prefer the authentic version.
Who can see my profile?+
Only verified employers on Mellow can view talent profiles.
